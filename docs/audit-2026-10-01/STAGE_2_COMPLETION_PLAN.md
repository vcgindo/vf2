## Honest disposition and final gate

Use the following distinctions inside existing MIGRATION rows, not a new ledger:

- **Verified contract:** named behavior and proof, with its data/actor scope.
- **Inherited defect reproduced:** evidence attributes it to the unchanged legacy
  implementation. Matching that defect can establish a narrow compatibility fact;
  it does not make a required working feature complete or approve a waiver.
- **Intentional divergence:** for example modern 503 versus legacy silent-empty.
  It is NOT parity. Document existing containment but do not silently approve it,
  change it to conceal failure, or repair legacy. Present a precise human decision
  if the required acceptance cannot otherwise be met.
- **Blocked dependency:** exact absent fact/provider/authority, affected exit,
  evidence and minimal unblock action. Do not infer absent facts from missing routes.
- **Untested/incomplete:** feasible work still owed; not a synonym for blocked.
